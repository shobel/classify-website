How it works

From roster to final placement in three steps.

Classify guides your team through a clear, repeatable process every year — no consultants, no setup headaches.

i.

Import your roster

Export a CSV from PowerSchool, Infinite Campus, Aeries, or any student information system. Classify walks you through mapping your columns — no reformatting required.

ii.

Set your priorities

Tell Classify what matters most. Adjust the weight of each factor, mark hard constraints like friendship and separation pairs, and add any school-specific attributes.

iii.

Run, review, and adjust

One click runs the optimizer. Review a full balance report across every class, then drag and drop students to make final tweaks. Export when you're done.

Features

Everything the process actually requires.

Built specifically for class placement — not a generic tool wedged into an educational context.

Built-in student attributes

Gender, behavior, independence, IEP, 504 plan, ESL, GATE, math level, and reading level are all built in and ready to optimize from day one. No configuration needed.

Friendship & separation rules

Mark students who need to be together or kept apart. These are treated as hard constraints — the optimizer will never violate them, regardless of what else it's balancing.

Custom variables

Every school tracks students differently. Add your own attributes — boolean flags or multi-value categories — and Classify will treat them exactly like built-in fields.

Adjustable weights

Control how much the optimizer cares about each factor. If gender balance matters more than math level at your school, say so — the optimizer responds to your priorities.

Year-to-year transitions

When the new school year starts, Classify promotes every student to their next grade automatically, preserving all their data, notes, and history. Start fresh without starting over.

Multi-user collaboration

Your whole team reviews placements together. Edit locks and real-time sync mean everyone sees the same list — no conflicting versions, no emailing spreadsheets back and forth.

Balance report

See a detailed breakdown of every factor across every class. A defensible, transparent result you can show parents, teachers, or your principal without hesitation.

Full audit history

Every change — who made it, what they changed, when — is logged. Full accountability without any extra effort from your team.

Privacy & security

Your student data never leaves your school.

This is what makes Classify genuinely different. Most software stores your data on someone else's servers. Classify doesn't — because it runs on yours.

Classify server

Installed on one computer at your school — a Mac or Windows machine the admin already has.

All student data lives here
School network only
No internet required

Teachers & staff

Open Classify in any browser on the school network. No app to install. No accounts to create on our end.

Browser-based access

No cloud, no breach risk from our side

We never receive your student records. If our servers were ever compromised, there would be nothing there to expose. Your data is only as vulnerable as your own network.

Role-based access control

Admins control who has access and what they can do. Teachers can view and collaborate; only admins can manage users, configure grades, and change school-wide settings.

Password-protected accounts

Every user has their own credentials. Admins invite staff via one-time codes — there's no self-registration. Accounts can be removed instantly when staff leave.

FERPA-friendly by design

Because student data never leaves your premises, there's no third-party vendor to sign a data processing agreement with. Your IT and legal teams can rest easy.

Full audit log

Every action — roster changes, placement runs, manual adjustments — is recorded with a timestamp and the user who made it. Complete accountability for any review.

No dependency on our uptime

Classify works entirely offline once installed. If we ever shut down, your installation keeps running forever. You own the software — it doesn't rent you access.

Requirements

What you need to get started.

Classify runs on one computer at your school. Everything else just needs a browser.

The Classify computer

One Mac or Windows PC

Classify installs on a single computer — typically the admin's machine or a dedicated office computer. It runs quietly in the background and doesn't require anything special.

  • macOS 12 or later, or Windows 10/11
  • Standard administrator account
  • Should stay on during the school day so teachers can connect
  • Internet only needed for initial activation and update checks
Teacher devices

Any device, any browser

Teachers open Classify in a browser — no app to install. Works on Macs, Windows PCs, Chromebooks, iPads, and phones.

  • Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge
  • Must be on the same school network as the Classify computer
  • No software installation required
Managed networks: On some enterprise-managed school networks, the easy-access hostname (classify.local) may be blocked. Teachers can still connect using the direct IP address instead — or your IT team can add a simple DNS entry. The app itself is unaffected either way.
