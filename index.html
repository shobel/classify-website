How it works

Three steps to balanced classes.

Classify guides you from raw roster to final placement with a clear, repeatable process your whole team can follow.

i.

Import your roster

Drop in a CSV from PowerSchool, Infinite Campus, Aeries, or any spreadsheet. Classify walks you through mapping your columns and values — no formatting required.

ii.

Configure your rules

Tell the optimizer what matters most. Set priority weights for each factor, mark hard constraints, and flag friendship pairs and conflicts per student.

iii.

Run and review

One click. The optimizer runs, shows you a balance report across every class, and lets you drag-and-drop students to make final adjustments.
